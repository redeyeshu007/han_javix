Handoverly AI – Builder Handover OS 
1. Product Overview 
Handoverly AI is a builder project-completion, customer-handover, defect-resolution, warranty, 
and association-transition platform. 
It helps builders manage the complete process from the stage when a property is nearing 
completion until the unit and common areas are officially handed over. 
Main product promise 
Complete property handovers faster, close defects transparently, organise every 
document, and transfer the completed project without missing responsibilities. 
 
2. Problem Handoverly Solves 
Many builders currently manage handovers using: 
●​ WhatsApp groups 
●​ Excel sheets 
●​ Paper checklists 
●​ Phone calls 
●​ Separate folders 
●​ Personal employee knowledge 
●​ Unorganised customer messages 
●​ Different contractor records 
Because of this, builders face problems such as: 
●​ Customers not knowing the handover status 
●​ Missing property documents 
●​ Unresolved defects 
●​ Repeated customer complaints 
●​ Delayed contractor work 
●​ No clear responsibility 
●​ Missed warranty dates 
●​ Missing common-area documents 
●​ Confusion during association transfer 
●​ Poor customer experience 
●​ Difficulty proving that work was completed 
Handoverly brings these activities into one organised system.

4. Product Boundaries 
Handoverly is not initially intended to be: 
●​ Complete construction accounting software 
●​ Full material inventory software 
●​ Employee payroll software 
●​ Complete civil-engineering design software 
●​ Property-listing marketplace 
●​ Broker-management platform 
●​ Full resident-community application 
Handoverly focuses primarily on: 
Project completion, unit inspection, customer handover, defect resolution, warranty, 
and builder-to-association transition. 
After handover, daily resident and association operations can continue through .

20. Conditional Handover 
Sometimes a customer may accept the property while a few minor items remain. 
In this case: 
●​ The customer accepts the unit conditionally. 
●​ Remaining items are clearly listed. 
●​ Each pending item has a deadline. 
●​ The responsible employee or contractor is assigned. 
●​ The customer receives progress updates. 
●​ Final closure occurs after all commitments are completed. 
Critical defects should not be allowed under conditional handover.

33. Important Business Rules 
Rule 1 
A unit cannot be marked customer-inspection ready before required internal inspections are 
completed. 
Rule 2 
A contractor cannot approve their own work. 
Rule 3 
Critical defects must be closed before final handover. 
Rule 4 
Only accounts employees or authorised management can provide financial clearance. 
Rule 5 
A customer handover must record keys, documents, assets, readings, and signatures. 
Rule 6 
A closed defect can be reopened with a valid reason. 
Rule 7 
The system must maintain a history of major actions. 
Rule 8 
Deleting an employee must not delete their completed work history. 
Rule 9 
The association cannot complete final acceptance while mandatory handover items remain 
unresolved. 
Rule 10 
 transfer should happen only after authorised association acceptance.
